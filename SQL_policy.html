Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SQLQuest — SQL Developer Learning Studio handles information when you use the SQLQuest web application.

1. About SQLQuest

SQLQuest is an educational, browser-based SQL learning application. It provides SQL lessons, practice questions, exams, a SQL worksheet/simulator, database exercises, and related learning tools.

SQLQuest is primarily a client-side web application. You do not need to create an SQLQuest account to use the learning features.

2. Information stored in your browser

SQLQuest uses browser storage to preserve application state, including learning or worksheet-related data and the simulated database state needed by the app. This may use technologies such as localStorage.

This browser-stored information is associated with your browser and device, not with an SQLQuest account. Clearing the site's browser storage, using private/incognito browsing, changing browsers, or using another device can remove or separate that locally stored state.

SQLQuest does not intentionally use browser storage to collect passwords, payment-card information, or other sensitive credentials.

3. Advertising and Monetag

SQLQuest may display advertising supplied by third-party advertising partners, including Monetag. Advertising providers may use technologies such as cookies, local storage, device/browser information, IP address, identifiers, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, secure, or improve advertising, subject to the provider's own policies and applicable law.

Monetag's publisher documentation confirms that its advertising integrations use site-specific advertising code and that its web-app integrations require JavaScript. SQLQuest does not control the data practices of third-party advertising providers.

For information about Monetag's current publisher and advertising requirements, consult the official Monetag documentation and policies applicable to your advertising integration.

Important: The exact cookies, identifiers, vendors, retention periods, and processing purposes used by an advertising network can change. This policy therefore does not promise that a particular cookie or identifier will always be used or never be used.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

SQLQuest itself may use browser storage for application functionality. Third-party advertising or embedded services may also use cookies or similar technologies. These technologies can be used for functions such as remembering settings, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, or delivering advertising.

You can manage or block cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect advertising or portions of the application's functionality.

5. External links and services

SQLQuest may provide links or actions that open external services, such as Google Search, GitHub Pages, or advertising-provider resources. When you leave SQLQuest or interact with an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

SQLQuest does not control the privacy practices, cookies, tracking technologies, content, or availability of external websites.

6. Google Search

If you use SQLQuest's Google Search feature, your search query is sent to Google through a Google Search URL. Google may process the query, browser/device information, IP address, cookies, and other information according to Google's applicable policies and your Google settings.

SQLQuest does not receive or store your Google Search account information merely because you opened a Google Search link from the application.

7. Information SQLQuest does not intentionally request

SQLQuest does not require users to submit a name, email address, telephone number, payment information, or government identification to use the core educational application.

Do not enter real passwords, authentication secrets, payment-card numbers, API keys, or other confidential information into SQL examples or practice data. SQLQuest is an educational simulator, not a secure secrets-management system.

8. How information may be used

  • To operate and preserve the SQLQuest application state in your browser.
  • To provide SQL lessons, exercises, exams, worksheet execution, and simulations.
  • To display and measure advertising where advertising is enabled.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and support reliable service operation.
  • To respond to technical or service requests if you voluntarily contact the project owner.

9. Third-party privacy practices

Advertising networks and other third-party services operate independently. Their collection and processing of information is governed by their own policies. Users should review those policies for current information about cookies, advertising identifiers, personalized advertising, retention, and available privacy controls.

For Google advertising services, Google explains that partner sites and apps can share information such as the URL and IP address with Google and that cookies may be used for advertising and measurement. Google also provides controls for advertising personalization and privacy choices.

10. Children's privacy

SQLQuest is an educational web application. It does not knowingly require children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the project directly, please contact the project owner so the situation can be reviewed.

11. Data security

SQLQuest uses HTTPS when served from its HTTPS hosting environment and follows reasonable application practices for a static educational web application. However, no website or browser storage mechanism can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Do not use SQLQuest to store production credentials, confidential company data, personal secrets, or other information requiring a controlled enterprise security environment.

12. Your choices

  • Manage or block cookies through your browser.
  • Clear SQLQuest's site data/local storage through your browser controls.
  • Use private/incognito browsing where supported.
  • Use available controls provided by advertising providers for personalized advertising.
  • Choose not to use external search or third-party links from SQLQuest.

Privacy controls provided by a third-party advertising provider may vary by country, browser, device, and applicable law.

13. International users and legal rights

Privacy and data-protection rights vary by jurisdiction. Where applicable, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, or consent withdrawal. The availability and scope of these rights depends on the law applicable to the user and the relevant processing activity.

If you need to exercise a legal privacy right relating specifically to SQLQuest, use the project contact channel associated with the website or its hosting repository.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SQLQuest's functionality, advertising partners, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.

15. Contact and project information

SQLQuest is published as a web application at SQLQuest — SQL Developer Learning Studio.

For project-specific questions, use the contact or issue mechanism made available with the SQLQuest project repository/hosting service.
