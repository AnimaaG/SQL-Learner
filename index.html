Privacy Policy

Ultimate MathSolverAI — Last updated: 29 September 2025

This privacy policy explains how the Ultimate MathSolverAI mobile application ("the App") handles user data and device information. The App is designed to work completely offline and does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any external servers by default.

1. Offline-first & Local Storage

The App runs entirely on the user's device. Any settings, preferences, saved problems, or user-created content are stored locally on the user's device only. We do not access, transmit, or store this data on our servers.

2. No data collection by the developer

We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, analytics, or any usage statistics from your device. There is no automatic upload of user data to any third party or to us.

3. Optional user-initiated backup

If you choose to create a backup of your app data (for example by exporting a file or using your Google account / Android Backup/Drive), this action is initiated and controlled by you. The developer does not trigger or access these backups. When you use a third-party backup mechanism (Google Drive, etc.), that third party’s terms and privacy policy apply.

Note: Backups or exports are optional. Only do this if you wish to preserve data between devices. We recommend reviewing the backup service’s privacy policy before storing sensitive content.

4. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions strictly required for its offline features (for example, file read/write if you use the import/export feature or camera permission if the app includes an optional camera-based math input). Permissions are used only on-device and solely for the feature you enable. You can revoke any permission at any time via your device settings.

5. Children

If your App is directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your territory), please ensure you comply with local laws (such as COPPA in the U.S.) and Play Console policies. The App does not knowingly collect information from children; if you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us (see contact section) so we can delete it.

6. Third-party services

The App does not bundle analytics, advertising SDKs, or other third-party trackers by default. If, in a future update, we integrate an online feature, analytics, or ads, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the Play Store listing will be amended to disclose those changes.

7. Security

Because data is stored locally, security depends on the safety of the user’s device. We recommend using device-level protections (PIN, fingerprint) and keeping regular backups if desired.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in the App. If we add any online features or data collection, we will notify users via the Play Store listing and update this page with the new date.
